ZikWall converts the original CSV into sorted compact IPv4/IPv6 range tables and an
ASN name table. Organisation names are shortened by removing legal suffixes
(for example "Amazon.com, Inc." becomes "Amazon") and a few well-known aliases.
The source and generated hashes are recorded in provenance.json. The owner is
the network that announces the address, not necessarily the service you reached:
cloud and CDN providers host many unrelated sites. The data is looked up locally;
no observed IPs are sent to DB-IP.
