ZikWall converts the original CSV into sorted compact IPv4/IPv6 range tables.
The source and generated hashes are recorded in provenance.json. Country labels
are approximate IP geolocation and may be wrong for VPNs, proxies, CDNs and other
networks. The data is looked up locally; no observed IPs are sent to DB-IP.
